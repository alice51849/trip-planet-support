What is Trip Planet?

Trip Planet is an iPhone app for parents that turns family travel into small missions a child can finish. Long flights, car rides and hotel stays become real-world quests such as trying a new food, being brave at the airport, saying thank you, spotting three colors at a museum, taking a memory photo or helping pack the hotel bag. Parents create a trip and choose Mission Packs for flight days, hotels, food, museums, kindness, bravery and memories; the missions appear as a glowing constellation map over animated space backgrounds, or over your own trip photo. Completed missions unlock rewards for a collectible gallery, each finished trip earns a Travel Passport stamp, and Trip Story Cards are generated to keep and share.

Who is Trip Planet for?

Parents travelling with children, whether on a flight, a road trip, a hotel stay or a weekend away. Two parents can share one trip, each from their own device, and iCloud co-parent sharing also includes grandparents. With Plus, one family can set up multiple children.

How does Trip Planet charge?

Free download with an in-app purchase. You can start free with 1 trip and 4 missions. Plus, an in-app purchase that sits behind a parent gate, unlocks more trips and missions, multiple children and premium theme worlds. There is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is Trip Planet different from other kids' travel apps?

Based on what the listing describes: instead of another checklist, kids get small real-world missions tied to the trip, with a constellation map, collectible rewards, passport stamps and story cards; and two parents can run the same trip from separate devices through iCloud. The listing also states that the app has no ads, no third-party tracking and no external servers, that purchases and sharing sit behind a parent gate, and that child names and photos stay on the device and in the family's iCloud.
