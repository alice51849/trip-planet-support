Kids travel games for the plane: set it up before the trip, play with no Wi-Fi

Plane Wi-Fi is often slow, paid or missing, and a long flight is the worst time to discover that the game you planned needs a connection. The easy fix is to do the setup at home, before the trip. In Trip Planet you create the trip and pick Mission Packs while you still have internet; on the plane your child finishes missions, collects rewards and works toward a passport stamp, with or without Wi-Fi.

Five minutes before you leave home

  1. Install Trip Planet on the device your child will use on the plane.
  2. Create the trip. Use an animated space background or your own trip photo.
  3. Choose Mission Packs. There are packs for flight days, hotels, food, museums, kindness, bravery and memories.
  4. Share with your co-parent now. Two parents can share one trip, each from their own device, through iCloud. Sharing needs a connection, so do it at home.
  5. Buy or restore Plus at home if you want it. Purchases also need a connection and sit behind a parent gate.

Frequently asked questions

Does Trip Planet work on a plane without Wi-Fi?

Yes, once it is set up. Install the app, create the trip and choose Mission Packs while you still have internet. Trips, missions, rewards and passport stamps are saved on the device, so on the plane your child can finish missions and collect rewards without a connection.

What needs an internet connection?

Sharing a trip with a second parent through iCloud, and buying or restoring Plus. Do those before you leave home.

What is free?

You can start free with 1 trip and 4 missions. Plus, an in-app purchase behind a parent gate, unlocks more trips and missions, multiple children and premium theme worlds. No subscription.

Does Trip Planet have ads or tracking?

Trip Planet has no ads, no third-party tracking and no external servers.

Can two parents manage the same trip?

Yes. Two parents can share one trip, each from their own device, using iCloud co-parent sharing.
