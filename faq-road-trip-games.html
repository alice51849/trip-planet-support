Trip Planet · FAQ

Long road trip with kids — what can they do in the car?

Trip Planet turns long flights, car rides and hotel stays into small missions your child can finish, with collectible rewards and a passport stamp for every completed trip. It gives kids something to work toward during the drive; how much screen time is up to you.

  1. Create a trip for your road trip.
  2. Choose Mission Packs — small real-world quests such as trying a new food, saying thank you, spotting colors or taking a memory photo.
  3. Your child’s missions appear as a glowing constellation adventure map.
  4. Each finished mission unlocks rewards for the collectible gallery; a completed trip earns a Travel Passport stamp.
  5. Afterwards, share an auto-generated Trip Story Card as a keepsake. Two parents can share one trip, each from their own device.

No ads and no third-party tracking. Purchases and sharing sit behind a parent gate.
